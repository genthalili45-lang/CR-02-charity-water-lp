The problem

Every day, women and kids walk for water.

In many communities, the nearest water source is hours away — and the water isn't even clean. That walk steals time from school, work, and dreams. It's a cycle that keeps whole communities stuck.

But clean water flips everything. Kids go back to class. Parents start businesses. Health improves overnight. One well can rewrite the future of an entire village.
